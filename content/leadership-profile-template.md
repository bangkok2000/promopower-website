# Leadership profile template (About Us)

Use this template for each person in the Leadership selector on `/about-us`. Copy must be **fact-checked and approved** by the individual before publish. Do not invent employers, qualifications, or client names.

**Data file:** `components/AboutUsContent.tsx` (`leadership` array)  
**UI:** `components/LeadershipSelector.tsx`

---

## Required fields (code)

| Field | Notes |
|--------|--------|
| `name` | Preferred public name |
| `role` | Job title as listed internally / on MOM records where applicable |
| `photo` | Path under `public/`, or `null` until approved headshot |
| `bio` | **Array of 2–3 paragraphs** (see length below) |
| `linkedIn` | Optional public profile URL |

---

## Copy structure (each member)

**Paragraph 1 — Role and tenure (40–55 words)**  
Title, scope at PromoPower, and **verified** start date or years with the company.

**Paragraph 2 — Operational accountability (45–60 words)**  
What they are responsible for in plain language: clients, recruitment, rosters, field support, multi-site campaigns, etc. Tie to how buyers experience the service.

**Paragraph 3 — Optional credibility (35–50 words)**  
Only **verified** education, certifications, or professional development—or a sentence on working style and stakeholders (field teams, vendors, clients). Omit if nothing is confirmed.

**Total target:** ~120–165 words across paragraphs.

**Do not include:** Client logos or campaign names without written approval; unverified degrees; hype (“visionary”, “industry-leading”).
