PROMOPOWER WEBSITE TRANSFORMATION BRIEF

Project Objective

Transform the new PromoPower website into a modern, professional, trust-building corporate website that reflects the company’s experience, operational excellence and long-standing reputation in providing staffing solutions for promotions, retail activations, events and customer engagement programmes.

The new website should maintain the premium visual design currently under development while significantly strengthening the editorial content, information architecture and conversion strategy.

The website must communicate the following core message:

PromoPower helps organisations execute successful customer-facing campaigns by providing professional staffing solutions supported by proven recruitment, training, deployment and management processes.

The website should position PromoPower as:

* Experienced
* Trustworthy
* Professional
* Operationally capable
* Service-oriented
* Responsive
* Consultative
* Results-focused

The website should not position PromoPower as:

* A creative agency
* An experiential agency
* A marketing consultancy
* A startup
* A lifestyle brand

PromoPower’s strength lies in its ability to consistently provide quality people supported by operational excellence.
