PROMOPOWER BRAND EDITORIAL & CONTENT STYLE GUIDE v1.0

Purpose

This document defines the voice, tone, messaging principles and writing standards for all PromoPower communications.

It applies to:

* Website content
* Service pages
* Landing pages
* Blog articles
* Sales collateral
* Marketing materials
* Social media content
* AI-generated content
* Future website updates

The purpose is to ensure consistency, professionalism and credibility across all written communications.

WRITING STRUCTURE

Whenever possible:

1. Introduce the challenge.
2. Explain why it matters.
3. Explain how PromoPower helps.
4. Explain the benefit.

This structure should guide most content.

⸻

EXAMPLE

Instead of:

“We provide professional promoters.”

Write:

“Effective customer engagement begins with having the right people representing your brand. Our professional promoters help organisations communicate product benefits, answer customer questions and create meaningful interactions that support campaign objectives.”

STORYTELLING FRAMEWORK

Where appropriate, include practical observations.

Example:

“A retail activation operating across multiple locations requires a very different staffing approach from a luxury beauty promotion or a corporate roadshow. Understanding these differences helps ensure the right people are deployed in the right environments.”

This demonstrates expertise without boasting.

SOCIAL PROOF RULES

Do not invent:

Statistics

Client counts

Revenue figures

Success rates

Testimonials

Case studies

Campaign results

If data is unavailable, discuss experience and capability rather than creating unsupported claims.

⸻

COPYRIGHT & PRIVACY RULES

Do not reference clients unless approved.

Do not use client logos without permission.

Do not use identifiable images without appropriate rights.

Do not imply endorsements that do not exist.

When discussing industries, focus on experience rather than specific organisations.

⸻

AI CONTENT RULES

All AI-generated content must be reviewed against this document.

AI must not:

Introduce marketing hype.

Invent claims.

Invent statistics.

Invent testimonials.

Create exaggerated promises.

Replace practical language with agency jargon.

If AI-generated content sounds like a marketing agency, rewrite it.

If it sounds like an experienced business partner, keep it.

⸻

THE FINAL TEST

Before publishing any content, ask:

Does this sound experienced?

Does this sound trustworthy?

Does this sound practical?

Does this sound professional?

Would a procurement manager trust this?

Would a marketing manager trust this?

Would this still sound credible ten years from now?

If the answer is yes, the content is aligned with the PromoPower brand.
