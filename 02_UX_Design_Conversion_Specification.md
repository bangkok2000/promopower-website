PROMOPOWER WEBSITE UX, DESIGN & CONVERSION SPECIFICATION v1.0

Purpose

This document defines the user experience, visual hierarchy, content presentation, conversion strategy and design enhancements for the new PromoPower website.

It should be used alongside:

* 01_Strategy_and_Content_Blueprint.md

This document does not replace the Content Blueprint.

It explains how the content should be experienced by visitors.

⸻

DESIGN OBJECTIVES

The website should feel:

Professional

Established

Trustworthy

Experienced

Dependable

Premium

Corporate

Human

Approachable

The website should communicate:

“We have been doing this for a long time, we understand the operational realities of staffing and activations, and you can trust us with your brand.”

The website should avoid appearing:

Trendy

Overly creative

Startup-like

Agency-centric

Entertainment-focused

Event glamour-focused

⸻

USER JOURNEY

A first-time visitor should move through the following emotional journey:

Stage 1

What does PromoPower do?

↓

Stage 2

Do they have experience?

↓

Stage 3

Can they be trusted?

↓

Stage 4

How do they work?

↓

Stage 5

Why are they different?

↓

Stage 6

⸻

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The existing design already possesses:

* Modern typography
* Good spacing
* Premium visual style
* Strong motion design
* Clean layout

These strengths should remain.

The goal is enhancement, not redesign.

⸻

CONTENT PRESENTATION PRINCIPLES

Rule 1

Avoid large walls of text.

Long-form content is encouraged.

However, long-form content must be visually broken into:

* Subheadings
* Pull quotes
* Highlight blocks
* Statistic cards
* Process diagrams
* Content callouts

⸻

Rule 2

Every major section should contain:

Headline

Intro paragraph

Supporting narrative

Visual element

CTA or continuation cue

⸻

Rule 3

No section should exceed 4–5 lines of uninterrupted text.

Use visual rhythm.

COPYRIGHT & PRIVACY SAFE VISUAL STRATEGY

Avoid:

Client logos

Client campaign photos

Brand packaging

Identifiable individuals

Third-party copyrighted imagery

Celebrity imagery

Stock-model clichés

⸻

Preferred Assets

Custom illustrations

Abstract environments

Workforce diagrams

Activation process graphics

Industry iconography

Branded patterns

Minimalist photography

Environmental imagery without identifiable people

⸻

VISUAL STORYTELLING SYSTEM

Every page should contain a mix of:

40% Content

30% Visual Storytelling

20% Process Graphics

10% CTAs

The website should never feel text-heavy despite containing substantial content.

⸻

TYPOGRAPHY

Preserve existing typography system.

Use:

Large headlines

Comfortable reading widths

Generous line spacing

Strong visual hierarchy

Avoid:

Dense paragraph blocks

Small text

Crowded layouts

⸻

MOTION DESIGN

Keep existing premium motion design.

Enhance with:

Section reveals

Timeline animations

Card interactions

Process transitions

Subtle hover effects

Avoid excessive animation.

Motion should support understanding.

Not distract.

⸻

CONVERSION STRATEGY

The primary goal of the website is not immediate conversion.

The primary goal is trust.

Trust leads to enquiries.

Every page should answer:

What do they do?

Why should I trust them?

How do they work?

What makes them different?

How can I contact them?

⸻

SUCCESS TEST

When a visitor leaves the website they should think:

“These people clearly know what they are doing.”

“They understand staffing and activations.”

“They have done this for a long time.”

“They appear professional and reliable.”

“I should speak with them.”

If the design, content and user experience achieve these outcomes, the website is successful.
